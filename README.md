# codeflare-2.0

## Subject

Mythology and folklore.

## Plan: MythosMatch

A personality quiz that tells you which mythological god you're most like.

Answer 7 questions about your personality, and the quiz matches you to one of 8 gods from Greek, Norse, and Egyptian mythology. You'll get a result with your god's name, powers, and a personality description.

Simple, interactive, and fun. No accounts, no complex features—just click, discover your divine match, and share it with friends.
